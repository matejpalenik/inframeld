# Code of Conduct

Inframeld is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous community.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and assume good intent;
- focus criticism on ideas and code, never on people;
- welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- keep discussions relevant, evidence-based, and constructive; and
- respect project boundaries, privacy, and security reporting procedures.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and unwelcome sexualized or personal attention are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Security issues must follow the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue. Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
