# Evaluation and comparisons

Evaluation helps you compare how configurations answer questions against a known set of cases. For a handbook assistant, a case can pair a realistic support question with reviewed expectations and evidence.

## Review the cases

Generated cases are candidates for review. A frozen benchmark preserves the selected inputs so later comparisons have a stable basis. Changing a test or rubric creates new identifiable evaluation inputs.

## Compare fresh results

A comparison records fresh execution and the evidence needed to interpret it. Faithfulness and passage coverage answer different questions. Missing evidence can make a claim unscorable rather than successful.

## Decide what to release

Evaluation supports your decision. Scores do not grant publication authority or establish current production health. [Deployments and releases](/product/deployments-and-releases) explains that separate control.

Public evaluation operations are not exposed in this preview. [Evaluate answer quality](/guides/evaluate-answers) is the guide entry point for that delivery.
